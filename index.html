Offramp Squad does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information. The game runs entirely on your device.

1. Information we collect

None. Offramp Squad has no account system, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, no social features, and no server operated by the developer. The app does not request any Android permissions beyond those every app receives automatically.

2. Data stored on your device

Your game progress (coins, research, inventory, map discovery, quest progress, on-ramps, and settings) is saved in the app's private storage on your device only. It never leaves your device. Uninstalling the app deletes this data. The app does not use Android cloud backup for this data.

3. Third parties

The game is distributed through Google Play. Google may process data about app installs, updates, and crashes under Google's own privacy policy and your device settings. The developer does not add any third-party software development kits for advertising, analytics, or tracking.

4. Children

Offramp Squad is not directed at children under 13. Because the app collects no data from anyone, it collects no data from children.

5. Purchases

The game has no real-money purchases. All in-game currency is earned by playing.

6. Security

Since no personal data is collected or transmitted, there is no personal data to secure beyond your own device's protections.

7. Changes to this policy

Any change will be posted at this address with a new effective date.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to pagestowages@gmail.com.

Offramp Squad is an original game by Pages to Wages. This page is the full privacy policy for the Android release on Google Play.
